# Month 6 professional review matrix

Status: **Draft 0.2 — Ireland-first review instrument.** No row is approved
until a named, appropriately scoped reviewer records a decision.

## How to use this matrix

1. Record the reviewer, qualification, organisation, declared scope, conflict
   statement, and date.
2. Review only rows within that declared scope.
3. Check the exact parent-facing wording against the registered source.
4. Record risks, required revisions, source suitability, decision, and
   next-review trigger.
5. Never infer whole-pack approval from one specialist's review.

## Decision values

- **Approved within scope**
- **Approved within scope after listed changes**
- **Changes required**
- **Remove**
- **Outside reviewer scope**

## Release rule

The Month 6 pack can move beyond research-only draft status only when:

- all 14 claim rows have a documented in-scope decision;
- all critical and high risks are resolved and retested;
- freshness verification is complete;
- the source register, pack, matrix, and approval record share one version;
- named approvers and next-review dates are recorded;
- parent comprehension and accessibility results meet the agreed release gates.
