# Month 6 professional review pack

Status: draft review pack. Use with scoped professionals only. Participation
does not imply endorsement of DevelopMonth as a whole.

## Review purpose

Review the Month 6 prototype and support-pack content for safety, accuracy,
scope, source needs, jurisdiction, parent-facing clarity, and professional
boundaries.

Month 6 is currently draft content for research. It is not approved for parent
reliance.

## Reviewer scope

Each reviewer must state their scope before review.

Possible scopes:

- general child health;
- public health nursing;
- complementary feeding;
- choking prevention and safe food preparation;
- safer sleep;
- movement, positioning, and physical development;
- communication, play, and shared attention;
- parent wellbeing and family support;
- plain-language editorial review;
- privacy, child-data, and future personalisation boundaries.

One reviewer should not approve content outside their professional scope.

## Review questions

### Overall safety

1. Is any wording unsafe, misleading, too certain, or insufficiently qualified?
2. Could any parent interpret the content as diagnosis, assessment, treatment,
   monitoring, or professional advice?
3. Is urgent or professional signposting clear enough without causing panic?
4. Are there risks of false reassurance?
5. Are there risks of unnecessary anxiety or milestone pressure?

### Domain accuracy

For each claim within your scope:

- is it accurate enough for draft testing?
- what source is required?
- what wording needs qualification?
- what wording should be removed?
- what jurisdiction or local guidance is needed?
- what review frequency is appropriate?

### Parent-facing language

1. Does the tone feel warm, positive, and parent-centred?
2. Is the reading level suitable for busy parents on mobile?
3. Is variation in child development described safely?
4. Is signposting gentle and clear?
5. Are families with different structures, abilities, feeding choices, and
   support networks included?

### Aion and future personalisation

1. Does Aion clearly read as a future educational organiser?
2. Does it avoid sounding like surveillance, diagnosis, monitoring, or
   professional assessment?
3. Are parent choice and control explicit enough?
4. Are example summaries appropriately cautious?
5. What content should Aion never generate without human review?

Use one row per issue or claim.

| PR-001 | — | — | — | — | — | — | Pending |

Risk values:

- Critical: credible safety, privacy, false reassurance, or clinical-boundary
  risk.
- High: must resolve before further parent testing.
- Medium: confusing, incomplete, or too vague.
- Low: wording polish.

Decision values:

- Approved within scope.
- Approved within scope after listed changes.
- Changes required.
- Remove.
- Outside reviewer scope.

## Required reviewer output

At the end of review, record:

- reviewer name;
- qualification/organisation;
- declared scope;
- conflicts of interest;
- reviewed content version;
- reviewed date;
- source recommendations;
- required changes;
- decision within scope;
- next review date or trigger.

## Non-negotiable release blockers

Month 6 cannot move toward parent reliance if:

- feeding, choking, allergens, sleep, movement, urgent-care, or signposting
  content lacks scoped review;
- jurisdiction is not confirmed;
- source register is incomplete;
- reviewer decisions are undocumented;
- Aion examples imply diagnosis, assessment, monitoring, or clinical judgement;
- parent-facing copy hides draft or review status;
- critical or high findings remain unresolved.
