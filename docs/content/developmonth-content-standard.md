# DevelopMonth content standard

Status: draft editorial and safety standard.

Use this standard for every DevelopMonth support pack, prototype screen, Aion
example, research document, and future parent-facing summary.

## Core promise

DevelopMonth supports child development and parenting, month by month.

Every piece of content should help parents:

- understand the month they are in;
- notice positive growth without pressure;
- support everyday play, feeding, sleep, movement, communication, bonding, and
  family confidence;
- prepare better questions for professionals when useful;
- feel calmer, not more judged.

## Writing principles

### Lead with growth

Start with what may be emerging, what families can enjoy, and what practical
support can help this month.

Avoid leading with problems, deficits, red flags, or milestone pressure unless
the context is a clearly separated safety/signposting section.

### Use variation language

Prefer:

- “may”
- “might”
- “some children”
- “many families”
- “can help”
- “one option”
- “if useful”

Avoid:

- “should by now”
- “must”
- “normal/abnormal” without professional context
- “delayed”
- “failed”
- “behind”
- “fix”

### Keep parents in control

When describing journal, calendar, check-ins, summaries, saved questions, or
future personalisation, state that parents choose what they add.

Do not imply surveillance, passive monitoring, hidden learning, or automatic
assessment.

### Make Aion safe

Aion should be described as a future educational organiser or companion layer.

It may be framed as helping to:

- organise parent-chosen notes;
- summarise the month;
- surface relevant support-pack ideas;
- prepare questions for professionals;
- remind parents what they chose to save.

It must not be framed as:

- diagnosing;
- assessing development;
- treating concerns;
- monitoring a child;
- replacing professionals;
- guaranteeing outcomes;
- learning everything about a child.

### Keep signposting gentle

Use a three-level model:

1. **Keep supporting:** positive everyday support.
2. **Try this next:** practical activity, observation, or question.
3. **Ask for guidance:** contact an appropriate professional when something
   concerns the family, persists, changes, or feels unclear.

Urgent-care guidance must be jurisdiction-specific, sourced, reviewed, and
clearly separated from general development content.

## Required content structure

Every monthly support pack should include:

1. positive introduction;
2. what may be growing this month;
3. practical play and bonding ideas;
4. movement and communication support;
5. feeding and sleep support;
6. parent confidence and family rhythm;
7. questions to save;
8. gentle signposting;
9. safety and professional-boundary note;
10. source, review, jurisdiction, and version status.

## Safety boundaries

Every public or research-facing surface must make clear that DevelopMonth:

- provides educational and organisational support only;
- does not replace professional medical advice;
- does not provide diagnosis, treatment, developmental assessment, clinical
  judgement, or emergency guidance;
- should direct families to their GP, public health nurse, paediatrician,
  therapist, emergency services, or relevant professional when needed.

## Evidence and review requirements

No monthly support pack should be treated as parent-ready until:

- every material claim has a source-register entry;
- jurisdiction is confirmed;
- reviewers are named by scope;
- review decisions are recorded;
- limitations and uncertainty are documented;
- the content version matches the source register and approval record;
- a next review date is assigned.

## Inclusion and accessibility

Content should:

- use plain language;
- avoid colour-only meaning;
- avoid assumptions about family structure, gender, feeding choices, ability,
  housing, income, culture, or support network;
- include alternatives where possible;
- avoid shame or blame;
- be readable on mobile;
- work with screen-reader-friendly headings and labels.

## Content QA checklist

Before release or research testing, check:

- [ ] positive development leads;
- [ ] language avoids pressure and comparison;
- [ ] parent control is explicit;
- [ ] Aion is future-facing and bounded;
- [ ] no diagnosis, treatment, assessment, monitoring, or guarantees are
      implied;
- [ ] professional signposting is calm and clear;
- [ ] source, review, jurisdiction, and draft status are visible;
- [ ] no real child data is requested in static prototypes;
- [ ] forms and disabled actions do not imply live saving;
- [ ] mobile reading is comfortable;
- [ ] accessibility labels and form labels are present where relevant.
