## Source standards

Prefer:

- National public-health and health-service guidance
- Current professional clinical guidelines
- Relevant systematic reviews or consensus guidance
- Primary peer-reviewed evidence where necessary

Do not use as the sole basis for parent-facing guidance:

- Commercial parenting content
- Search-result summaries
- Unsourced blogs or social posts
- Generated summaries
- Guidance from another jurisdiction without explicit review
