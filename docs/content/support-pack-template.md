# Monthly support-pack template

Use this template for every DevelopMonth month. It establishes structure, not
clinical truth. Each pack requires evidence review and named editorial approval.

## Pack metadata

- Month:
- Working title:
- Parent-facing promise:
- Intended age context:
- Version:
- Last reviewed:
- Next review:
- Content owner:
- Professional reviewers:
- Jurisdiction/local guidance:

## Opening

### Positive introduction

Write 60–100 words about growth, connection, curiosity, play, family rhythms,
and parent confidence. Avoid comparison, certainty, and “should by now”.

### This month may include

Choose three to five themes. Use “may”, “might”, and “some children” where
variation is expected.

## 1. Development guidance

- What may be emerging
- What parents might notice
- How variation should be described
- Evidence/review sources

## 2. Play and connection

Include three to five low-cost, accessible, everyday ideas. Each idea should:

- Be optional
- Avoid performance targets
- Include supervision or positioning safety where relevant
- Work within ordinary routines
- Offer adaptations for different needs where possible

## 3. Movement and communication

Cover movement, sounds, gestures, shared attention, and early language without
using a milestone checklist as the emotional centre.

## 4. Feeding and sleep

Separate supportive family guidance from content requiring professional or
jurisdiction-specific review. Link to current authoritative guidance.

## 5. Parent confidence and family support

- What may feel difficult
- Ways to share care
- Low-pressure routines
- Support for the parent’s wellbeing
- Inclusive language for different families and carers

## 6. Questions to ask

Offer calm questions a parent can save for an appropriate professional. Do not
recommend a diagnosis or imply that asking a question confirms a problem.

## Gentle signposting

Use three levels:

1. **Keep supporting:** positive everyday support.
2. **Try this next:** practical activity, observation, or question.
3. **Ask for guidance:** contact an appropriate professional when something
   persists, changes, or concerns the family.

Urgent guidance must be sourced, jurisdiction-aware, reviewed, and clearly
separated from general development content.

## Accessibility and inclusion check

- Plain language
- Screen-reader-friendly structure
- No colour-only meaning
- No gender, family-structure, feeding-method, or ability assumptions
- Alternatives for movement, sensory, communication, and access needs
- Translation-ready wording

## Required final checks

- Positive development leads.
- Claims are evidence-aligned and appropriately qualified.
- No diagnosis, treatment, assessment, or guarantees.
- Professional-review items are approved.
- Sources and version are recorded.
- Safety statement is present but not dominant.
- Parent testing and professional testing feedback are resolved.
