# DevelopMonth MVP blueprint

## Product promise

DevelopMonth helps parents support and celebrate their child’s development,
month by month. It organises positive guidance, family notes, practical
activities, questions, and gentle signposting without turning childhood into a
scorecard.

## First release outcome

A parent should be able to answer three questions calmly:

1. What may be growing or changing this month?
2. What can we enjoy or try in everyday family life?
3. What would we like to remember or ask?

The first release is not a diagnostic, assessment, treatment, or clinical
record system.

## Core product areas

### ChildProfile

Stores the minimum information needed to organise age-relevant support. The
prototype uses only a fictional first name and month.

### This Month

The parent’s calm starting point. It introduces the monthly theme, a small
number of practical ideas, upcoming family moments, and links to the rest of
the system.

### Journal

Optional parent-authored notes about small wins, routines, questions, and
changes. It should feel like a family record, not surveillance.

### Calendar

Optional appointments, reminders, check-ins, and family activities. Calendar
items are not developmental deadlines.

### MonthlyCheckIn

A short reflection across connection, play, movement, communication, feeding,
sleep, and family support. Questions may be skipped. Answers are not scores.

### SupportPack

Positive month-specific guidance covering development, play, bonding,
movement, communication, feeding, sleep, parent confidence, and questions.

### DevelopMonthCompanion

A parent-controlled organisational layer that may summarise information a
parent chooses to provide. It must distinguish parent notes, published
guidance, and generated summaries. It must not diagnose or make clinical
claims.

### DevelopmentSummary

A warm monthly record made from parent-selected notes and completed product
activities. It is not a clinical record or developmental assessment.

### QuestionsForProfessional

A parent-owned list of calm questions for a GP, public health nurse,
paediatrician, therapist, or care team. Questions are preparation, not referral
decisions.

## Month 6 prototype journey

1. Parent opens **This Month** and sees “Starting solids and movement”.
2. Parent reads one positive idea for play and connection.
3. Parent opens **Journal** and sees how a small win could be captured.
4. Parent opens **Calendar** and sees family reminders alongside an appointment.
5. Parent previews a five-area **Check-in** framed as reflection, not a test.
6. Parent explores the full **Support Pack**.
7. Parent sees how the **Companion** could organise only selected information.
8. Parent previews a warm **Monthly Summary**.

## Content hierarchy

Every month should follow this emotional order:

1. Celebrate growth and connection.
2. Offer everyday support.
3. Build parent confidence.
4. Invite optional reflection.
5. Prepare calm questions.
6. Signpost only when useful.
7. Repeat safety boundaries without overwhelming the family experience.

## MVP boundaries

Not in the static prototype:

- Accounts or authentication
- Database or cloud storage
- Live personalisation
- AI or automated summaries
- Payments or subscriptions
- Messaging professionals
- Diagnosis, treatment, triage, or medical assessment
- Developmental scoring, ranking, or comparison

## Decision gates before implementation

1. Validate language and emotional safety with parents.
2. Validate clinical boundaries and signposting with relevant professionals.
3. Approve the content review and versioning workflow.
4. Complete a privacy impact assessment for child and family information.
5. Decide the minimum data needed for each product outcome.
6. Prototype deletion, export, consent, and personalisation controls.
7. Only then select authentication, database, hosting, and automation services.
